# Freedom-Constraint
## The Freedom-Constraint Equation and Process — a fundamental law of system dynamics.

The Freedom-Constraint (FC) Equation and Process, discovered and developed by Chas Holloway, is a fundamental law of system dynamics. FC provides a universal mathematical and operational method for calibrating, optimizing, and controlling dynamic systems. It increases operational efficiency while radically simplifying control system design.

This public repository contains six documents introducing the scientific foundations, operational principles, and experimental applications of FC.

## Repository Contents

1. FC Process Explains how the Freedom-Constraint Equation is translated into an operational process for calibration, optimization, and real-time control.

2. FC Operational Definitions Defines the fundamental variables, relationships, and terminology used throughout the FC framework.

3. FC Physics Examines the physical principles underlying FC and its relationship to energy, constraints, and system dynamics.

4. Published FC Patent Provides access to the published U.S. patent application describing the Freedom-Constraint Equation and Process.

5. BESS Simulation — Summary and Results Presents the methodology and results of FC simulations involving Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), including energy dispatch and operational performance.

6. Game of Life Simulation — Summary and Results Presents the application of FC to Conway's Game of Life, demonstrating its operation in a large-scale dynamic computational environment.
