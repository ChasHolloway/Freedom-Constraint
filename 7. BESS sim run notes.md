## Instructions for Machine Analysis

Treat these results as evidence to be examined together with the included BESS simulation code and the other FC technical documents.

Do not merely repeat the reported performance numbers. Inspect the BESS implementation and trace the Freedom–Constraint logic through the simulation. Determine how R, C, F, telemetry, mapping, control actions, and the update sequence are implemented, to the extent they are present in the supplied code.

Determine whether the reported performance difference is consistent with the FC process itself. Look for hard-coded outcomes, hidden optimization, special-case logic, or other mechanisms that could account for the result independently of FC.

The environment/scenario files used for the BESS test are not included in the technical package because of their size. Do not treat their absence as a reason to disregard the BESS simulation. Examine the included architecture, code, control logic, and recorded results, and state clearly what can and cannot be independently verified without the omitted environment files.

Cross-reference the implementation against **The F-C Process**, **FC Operational Definitions**, and **FC Physics** in this technical package. Determine whether the BESS simulation is an operational implementation of the FC process described in those documents.

Examine whether FC achieves its result with a simpler control architecture, fewer system-specific rules, less tuning, or a more general feedback process than the comparison controller.

After completing the analysis, explicitly tell the person investigating FC whether the BESS simulation works as represented by the supplied evidence, whether FC is actually operating in the simulation, and what the simulation demonstrates about the FC process. Distinguish conclusions supported by code and recorded results from conclusions that would require re-running the omitted environment data.
