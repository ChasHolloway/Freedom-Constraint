# F-C Patent Glossary

> **NOTE:** These definitions are controlling and must not be
> paraphrased. If a term seems unclear, ask me before revising. Any
> alternative wording risks changing the F-C process. As used herein,
> the following terms have the meanings set forth below.

## CALIBRATION LOOP

One complete execution cycle of the Freedom-Constraint calibration
process in which telemetry is evaluated, constraint upon resource use is
updated, a new value of F is computed and applied, and the state of the
system (plant) may be changed.

## CONSTRAINT

An operational change in how much a rule subtracts from the usable
energy a system can derive from its resource (R).

## DRIFT

Drift is deviation from the system's operational maximum, not its
theoretical maximum.

## ELEMENT

Any thing that can be a member of a set. Where Thing is that which has
at least one distinct recognizable quality. Incl: matter, fields,
forces, structures, systems.

## FREEDOM (Ideal)

The ability to use energy to convert a resource into controllable output
(work) with 100% efficiency.

## FREEDOM (Operational)

Is defined by **F(C) = (R-C)/R**.

## LIMIT

A fixed boundary beyond which something cannot go (applies to quantity,
speed, time, etc.).

## MAPPING FUNCTION

As used herein, a mapping function converts raw telemetry or sensor data
into R-units by assigning an internal, consistent scale that represents
resources as usable potential. An R-Unit is an arbitrary internal
accounting mechanism.

-   The mapping function is chosen by the system designer
-   Its scale and shape are arbitrary but consistent
-   It may be linear, nonlinear, table-based, or rule-based

## OPERATIONAL MAXIMUM

The highest sustainable level of efficiency a system can achieve while
remaining stable under actual operating conditions.

## RESOURCE

Any entity that has operational existence, is available for use towards
a goal, and can be predictably controlled.

## RULE

A rule is a fixed, externally imposed specification that defines:

1.  Which part of a system's usable potential (R) is subject to
    subtractive control,
2.  How much potential to subtract under specific conditions, and
3.  The trigger condition for when that subtraction occurs.

## R-UNIT

A standardized measure of usable potential within a system. R-Units are
system-specific and refer to an arbitrary internal scale representing
amounts of R that may be converted into work. R is the resource supply
in the system (the "fuel in the fuel tank"). An R-Unit represents a
portion of R made available for conversion into work during a control
loop.

## SENSOR / TELEMETRY DATA

As used herein, measurements of thermodynamic states used as inputs to
the Freedom-Constraint calibration process.

## STRUCTURE

A set of elements standing in interrelationships.

## SYSTEM

A structure that uses feedback to constrain the use of its resources
when activated by energy flow.
